Notes:

There are a lot of caviats to this data, especially in the case of Ultimates. Feel free to use this as a general priority, but keep in mind that this does not give perfect information.

At the end of the day you will get the best data for your group by analyzing your group's logs. The aim of this page is to get a general idea of how the jobs perform as card targets in individual fights.

Things to consider that this data does not display:

  • Does a window have multiple targets?
  • Does a player have dance partner?
  • Is there a gear discrepancy?
  • Player skill.

At the moment, I do not have a considerable number of reports logged for older ultimates. If more data is uploaded to the card calc, I will include them here.
